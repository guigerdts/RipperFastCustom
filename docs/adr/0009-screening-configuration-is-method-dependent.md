# Screening configuration is method-dependent

A screen is the binding of a method and its own parameters to one ink channel inside a profile.
AM needs frequency, angle and dot shape; other methods may need different fields, and none is
forced to declare LPI or angle. Validation follows the selected method.

**Considered options**: one AM-shaped configuration for every method (rejected — it would bake
AM concepts into FM and into methods that do not exist yet).
